Computer Science Department Tutorial

The Computer Science (CS) curriculum at Debark University is a rigorous four-year journey designed to transform students into skilled
logic-builders and software In the foundational years, students are introduced to the building blocks of the digital world through courses
like Programming Fundamentals (C++/Java), and Data Structures and Algorithms.

As students reach their "Half-Life" milestone, the complexity intensifies with systems-level thinking in Operating Systems, Database
Management, and Computer Architecture. The final stages of the program shift toward specialization, covering high-demand fields such
as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Network Security, and Mobile Application Development. This academic path culminates in a Senior
Capstone Project, where students apply their coding skills to solve real-world problems, often focusing on local challenges like
digitalizing agricultural data or improving healthcare systems in the North Gondar region.
