DKU Computer Science Student Half-Life Celebration

At Debark University (DKU), the "Half-Life" celebration is a high-energy milestone that turns the chilly highland campus into a center of warmth
and camaraderie. As students reach the midpoint of their rigorous degree programs—be it in the College of natural science department of Computer Science or the atmosphere shifts from quiet study to a festive parade. The day is defined by the sight of students clad in custom-designed
batch t-shirts, often adorned with the university's motto, br"Our Success Mount on Educational Quality," or witty slogans poking fun at the difficulty
of their specific majors. Against the backdrop of the misty Simien Mountains, students participate in vibrant parades, traditional Amhara dances like
the Eskista, and the iconic departmental "roasting," where each batch humorously defends their major’s superiority over others.

The celebration is not just a party; it is a psychological release for a community that often balances academic pressure with the unique challenges of
a high-altitude climate. For the students in the gateway city to Ras Dejen, "Half-Life" serves as a collective deep breath—a moment to reflect on
the modules they've passed and to build the stamina needed for the final , years of their journey.
